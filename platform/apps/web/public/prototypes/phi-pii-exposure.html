Method note

Classification uses agentless metadata only (buildDataAccessClassification): permissions, granted scopes, knowledge/vector/memory store names, connected apps, and explicit collector labels. Visentra does not open documents or read prompts to decide PHI/PII. PHI outranks PII when both are present (primaryDataClass).
